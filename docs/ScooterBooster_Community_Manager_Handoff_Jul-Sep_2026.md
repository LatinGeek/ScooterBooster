### Eliminación de límite de velocidad

**What it includes:** Firmware backup, limit adjustment, and post-change validation.

**Best content territories:** Model/battery/controller reality; why outcomes differ; what the legal acknowledgment means.

**Guardrail:** Never promise a specific final speed or suggest public-road use.

### Actualización de firmware

**What it includes:** Version review and backup, official or compatible custom update, configuration, and test.

**Best content territories:** Official vs. custom; error codes; when an update can restore functions.

**Guardrail:** Do not promise more autonomy or performance without a model-specific diagnosis.

### Control crucero

**What it includes:** Compatibility review, activation or hardware assessment, threshold setup, and cutoff test.

**Best content territories:** Comfort on longer trips; how activation/cutoff works; compatibility myths.

**Guardrail:** Do not frame as hands-free riding or a reason to reduce attention.

### Mantenimiento general

**What it includes:** Mechanical/electrical diagnosis, brakes, steering/folding, tires, and safety review.

**Best content territories:** Winter wear; noises/vibration; preventive care; pre-sale checklist.

**Guardrail:** Clarify that replacement parts and extra work require a separate quote.

**08 · EIGHT-WEEK CONTENT STRATEGY**

## The strategic shift

Keep deslimitación as the high-interest doorway, then move the audience into four broader ideas: model literacy, safety/maintenance, technician credibility, and booking clarity.

### Recommended content mix

| **Pillar** | **Share** | **Role** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model + service education | 30% | Searchable, saveable explanations tied to live catalog models |
| Maintenance + safety | 25% | Broaden relevance and build responsible brand trust |
| Technician + process | 20% | Human proof, behind-the-work, verification, and recruitment |
| Reviews + outcomes | 15% | Credibility, with exact wording and realistic qualifiers |
| Community prompts | 10% | Questions, polls, model requests, symptom triage, and UGC |

### Suggested weekly rhythm

- 2 Reels: one technician/process, one model or symptom explainer.

- 1 carousel: checklist, comparison, myth/fact, or step-by-step booking guide.

- 3-5 Story sequences: polls, question stickers, availability, FAQs, and post follow-ups.

- 1 community prompt: ask for a model, symptom, or misconception to fuel the next week.

### Creative system

**Voice:** Rioplatense Spanish, direct, technically confident, useful, and never macho or reckless.

**Visual anchors:** Emerald brand accent, close-up mechanical/firmware details, model names on screen, clear UYU amounts, real technician context.

**Hook formula:** Specific model or symptom + tension + honest constraint. Example: '¿Tu Xiaomi 5 quedó limitado después de una actualización?'.

**Proof formula:** Show the check, explain the decision, state what varies, and end with the correct booking step.

### Responsible-riding baseline

- For Montevideo, current departmental rule D.709.8 states a 25 km/h maximum for monopatines on public roads; D.709.7 directs them to the roadway or dedicated cycling infrastructure where present.

- Regulation varies by department. Social content should not translate a Montevideo rule into a nationwide legal conclusion.

- A current UNASEV report records VMP injury cases rising from 26 in 2020 to 191 in 2025 and identifies electric scooters as the category with the largest increase. This supports a strong safety/maintenance pillar.

- This guide is not legal advice. Final legal claims and disclaimers require review by qualified Uruguayan counsel.
